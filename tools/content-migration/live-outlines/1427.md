---- section stark-faq lt-blue-section
TEXT FAQ
TEXT Frequently asked questions
TEXT Clear answers to the practical questions that come up before a project begins.
LI How involved do I need to be? Focused, but not overwhelming. We’ll need your input on goals, priorities, and feedback at key points. Day-to-day execution, structure, and implementation are handled by us.
LI Do you write the website content? We help shape and refine content, but we don’t replace your voice. Depending on the project, we can work from existing copy, collaborate on new messaging, or coordinate with a copywriter.
LI How long does a typical website project take? Most projects take 6–10 weeks, depending on scope, feedback timing, and content readiness. We’ll outline a clear timeline before work begins.
LI Will my site be easy to update after launch? Yes. We build with clarity and longevity in mind, using tools that make updates straightforward without breaking layouts or structure.
LI What happens after the site goes live? We don’t disappear. After launch, we provide guidance, cleanup, and optional ongoing support so the site continues to perform and evolve.
LI What if we’re not a fit? We’ll be honest early. Strong work depends on alignment — if expectations or working styles don’t match, we’d rather say so than force it.
