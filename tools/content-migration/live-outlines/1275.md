---- section stark-fit lt-blue-section
TEXT Who It’s For
TEXT The difference isn’t effort. It’s structure, clarity, and restraint.
TEXT Web design works best when it’s treated like a system — not a one-time layout.
TEXT You’re a good fit if…
LI You want your website to clearly communicate what you do and who it’s for.
LI You care about structure, hierarchy, and how users move through a page.
LI You want a site that’s easy to update, scale, and maintain over time.
LI You value clarity and usability as much as visual design.
LI You see your website as a long-term foundation, not a one-time project.
TEXT This is probably not a fit if…
LI You want a website to look modern without defining what it needs to do.
LI You’re focused on visual trends more than usability or structure.
LI You expect design alone to fix deeper clarity or positioning issues.
LI You want something launched quickly without planning for growth or maintenance.
LI You’re treating your website as a brochure instead of a working system.

---- section stark-faq lt-blue-section
TEXT FAQ
TEXT Frequently asked questions
TEXT Clear answers to the practical questions that come up before a project begins.
LI How involved do I need to be? Focused, but not overwhelming. We’ll need your input on goals, priorities, and feedback at key points. Day-to-day execution, structure, and implementation are handled by us.
LI Do you write the website content? We help shape and refine content, but we don’t replace your voice. Depending on the project, we can work from your existing copy, collaborate on new messaging, or coordinate with a copywriter.
LI How long does a typical website project take? Most projects take 6–10 weeks, depending on scope, feedback timing, and content readiness. We’ll outline a clear timeline before we begin.
LI Will my site be easy to update after launch? Yes. We build with clarity and longevity in mind, using tools that make updates straightforward without breaking layouts or structure.
LI What happens after the site goes live? We don’t disappear. After launch, we provide guidance, cleanup, and optional ongoing support to ensure the site continues to perform and evolve.
LI What if we’re not a fit? We’ll be honest early. Good websites come from alignment — if expectations or working styles don’t match, we’d rather say so than force it.
