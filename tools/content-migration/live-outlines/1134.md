TEXT First 30 Days
TEXT What the first month looks like
TEXT A structured ramp that creates signal before scale.
TEXT We don’t launch ads just to “be running.” The first 30 days are about clean setup, controlled tests, and learning what actually moves results—so month two is built on proof, not guesses.
TEXT Week 1 — Foundation
TEXT Tracking, structure, and definitions
TEXT Confirm conversion events, attribution, access, and reporting. Define success metrics and guardrails before spend starts.
TEXT Week 2 — Build
TEXT Creative angles + audience hypotheses
TEXT Develop initial creative directions and audience segments. Build campaigns with intentional testing structure and pacing.
TEXT Week 3 — Launch
TEXT Controlled spend, real data
TEXT Go live with a measured budget. Validate tracking, watch early performance signals, and identify what’s worth iterating.
TEXT Week 4 — Optimize
TEXT Learnings → direction for scale
TEXT Refine based on signal: shift budget, improve creative, tighten targeting, and set the roadmap for next-month scaling.
TEXT By day 30, you have: a clean baseline, proven angles, and a clear plan for what to scale next.

---- section stark-faq
TEXT FAQ
TEXT Frequently asked questions
TEXT Straight answers to the questions clients usually ask before getting started.
LI How quickly will we see results? Paid advertising generates data quickly, but meaningful performance comes from iteration. Early weeks are about learning and signal; sustainable results follow as we refine what’s working.
LI How much budget do we need to start? Budgets are set based on goals, competition, and learning needs — not arbitrary minimums. We’ll recommend a starting range that allows for testing without unnecessary risk.
LI Do we control spend and decisions? Yes. You always know what’s running, why it’s running, and how much is being spent. Nothing scales without clarity and agreement.
LI What if ads don’t perform? That’s part of the process. Underperformance is treated as data, not failure. We adjust creative, targeting, and structure based on real signals instead of guesswork.
LI Is this a long-term commitment? No lock-ins. We prefer to earn trust through results and transparency, not contracts.
