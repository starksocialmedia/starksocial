---- section stark-faq lt-blue-section stark-section
TEXT FAQ
TEXT Frequently asked questions
TEXT Straight answers to the questions clients usually ask before getting started.
LI How involved do I need to be? Lightweight. You’ll review content, give feedback, and join occasional check-ins — but we handle the planning, execution, and day-to-day work.
LI Do you post without approval? No. Nothing goes live without your sign-off unless we agree otherwise. We’ll set a clear approval process upfront so there are no surprises.
LI How quickly will I see results? Social media is cumulative. Most brands see traction in the first few months, with stronger momentum building over time.
LI Is there a long-term contract? No long lock-ins. We prefer to earn the relationship by doing good work, not by trapping you in a contract.
LI What if we’re not a fit? We’ll tell you early. This only works when expectations, pace, and priorities are aligned.
