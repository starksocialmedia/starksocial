TEXT Nathan Imhoff
TEXT Co-Founder · Creative & Technical
TEXT Background
TEXT Nathan Imhoff is a co-founder of Stark Social Media Agency and leads creative direction across web, brand, and visual systems. His work focuses on clarity, structure, and execution—turning complex ideas into clean, usable design that holds up over time.
TEXT Nathan’s path into design started early through self-taught coding, building his own computers, and hands-on experimentation. That foundation carried into formal training, freelance work, agency roles, and city government, including serving as a Graphic & Web Designer for the City of Santa Clarita on nationally recognized projects.
TEXT Good design should reduce friction. For Nathan, layout, hierarchy, and structure are functional decisions with long-term consequences. If a site is difficult to update, confusing to navigate, or fragile to maintain, the design has failed—regardless of how it looks at launch.
TEXT His commitment to quality work and community involvement has been recognized with the LA County Sheriff’s Department Star Scroll , and he was selected as one of Santa Clarita’s 40 Under Forty for his dedication to excellence and service.
TEXT At Stark, Nathan leads creative with a practical standard: design should make execution easier, not harder. The systems he builds are meant to be understood, maintained, and trusted—by clients and internal teams alike.
TEXT Nathan continues to refine his craft through ongoing study and hands-on work, bringing together design, technology, and storytelling to create experiences that are intentional, durable, and built to last.

TEXT Nathan approaches creative work as a system—not decoration. His focus is on building design and web foundations that are clear, intentional, and durable, so teams can grow without constantly rebuilding.

TEXT Design sites that prioritize clarity, hierarchy, and real user behavior—not trends.
