---- section stark-faq
TEXT FAQ
TEXT Frequently asked questions
TEXT Clear answers to the questions clients usually ask before getting started.
LI What kind of content do you create? We focus on strategic content that supports clear messaging and long-term goals — not one-off posts or trend chasing. The format depends on what actually serves your audience and objectives.
LI How involved do we need to be? Collaborative, but not heavy. We’ll align on direction and review work at key points, while we handle planning, production, and day-to-day execution.
LI Do you post content without approval? No. Nothing goes live without your sign-off unless we agree on a different process upfront.
LI How do you measure success? We look beyond surface metrics. Success is defined by clarity, consistency, and whether the content supports broader marketing and business goals.
LI Is this ongoing or project-based? Both can work. Some teams need ongoing support, others need a strong foundation. We’ll recommend what makes sense based on your situation.
